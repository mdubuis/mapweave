Mira Thorne runs the harbor at [[Old Port]] the way some rulers run a kingdom: through fees,
favors, and a very long memory. She has never held a formal title in the
[[Silver Compact]], which is exactly how she prefers it — titles can be stripped, but nobody has
found a way to un-appoint the person everyone already asks first.

She is rumored to have been present at the opening skirmishes of [[The Salt War]], though she
denies it whenever asked directly.
