Three years after [[The Salt War]] officially ended, its old raiding bands haven't gotten the
message. [[Mira Thorne]] wants a tithe of salt moved out of [[Old Port]] before the
[[Silver Compact]]'s rivals can claim it "went missing" again.

## GM notes

- The crossing encounter can be resolved with the delta-crossing encounter table.
- If the party stalls past three in-game days, Mira raises the pay but also raises her suspicion
  that they're stalling for a better offer elsewhere.
