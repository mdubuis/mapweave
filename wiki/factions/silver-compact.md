---
title: Silver Compact
type: faction
summary: A merchant league that governs trade along the Silt Delta, not the land itself.
tags: [merchant-league, delta]
---

The Silver Compact is not a state and claims no territory — it is an agreement between the
trading houses of the delta to enforce shared weights, shared tolls, and a shared fleet against
piracy. [[Old Port]] is its unofficial capital, for no better reason than that most of its members
keep warehouses there.

[[Mira Thorne]] is, in practice if not on paper, the closest thing the Compact has to a leader.

The Compact's neutrality was tested hard during [[The Salt War]], when both sides tried to buy its
fleet out from under the other.
