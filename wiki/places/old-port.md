Old Port sits where the Silt river meets the sea, its docks crowded with the boats of a dozen
minor states. No single power has ever managed to hold it for long — the town changes hands on
paper more often than in practice, and the dockworkers barely notice when it does.

The [[silver-compact|Silver Compact]] keeps its counting-house here, more out of convenience than
loyalty: whoever controls Old Port's harbor fees controls a slice of every trade route on the
delta.

[[Mira Thorne]] was born in the shanty district before she ever set foot on a ship.
