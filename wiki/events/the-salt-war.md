The Salt War was never declared and never formally ended — two rival claimants to the salt pans
upstream of [[Old Port]] spent three years burning each other's warehouses instead of meeting in
open battle.

The [[Silver Compact]] stayed neutral in name, though its fleet was chartered by both sides at
different points, at a price nobody involved has ever admitted to paying. [[Mira Thorne]]'s exact
role remains a subject of dockside gossip rather than record.
